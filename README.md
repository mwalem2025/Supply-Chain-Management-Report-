# Supply-Chain Management Report 
#### Supply Chain DashBoard 

## Project Overview 
This project focuses on developing an interactive supply chain dashboard designed to provide a clear, data-driven view of business performance. The primary objective is to identify opportunities for cost reduction and supplier efficiency improvements, ultimately enabling more informed decision-making and enhancing overall profitability. The dashboard will serve as a central analytical tool that transforms raw supply chain data into actionable insights. It will highlight key performance indicators (KPIs) such as supplier lead times, order accuracy, transportation costs, inventory turnover, and procurement efficiency. By continuously monitoring these metrics, the business can pinpoint inefficiencies, reduce waste, and negotiate more effectively with suppliers. A core feature of the dashboard is its interactive and flexible reporting capability. Users will be able to drill down into specific product categories for a more granular analysis. The dashboard will focus on three high-level categories:

Cosmetics 
Haircare 
Skincare (e.g., moisturizers, serums, cleansers)

This categorical breakdown allows stakeholders to compare performance across product lines, identify which segments are driving costs or inefficiencies, and tailor improvement strategies accordingly. For example, the dashboard could reveal that while skincare suppliers are highly reliable, haircare suppliers show frequent delays—prompting a focused review of that category.

By combining high-level KPI tracking with deep, category-specific drill-downs, the dashboard empowers supply chain managers, procurement teams, and business leaders to make precise, fact-based decisions that reduce operational costs, increase supplier accountability, and directly improve the company’s bottom linel.
